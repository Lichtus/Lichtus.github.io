About

I write Python and build things that actually run: Streamlit applications, predictive models, integrations with external APIs and n8n workflows with AI agents inside them.

Most of my work is tooling for manufacturing companies — customer-facing systems, quoting calculators and sales automation. I design and write the solution myself, from the data model up to the interface.

In practice that means wiring scattered tools into one working ecosystem: an API, a database, a language model and a simple interface that someone actually uses.
